# ITPM - Introduction to Agile [101-Basics]

## Topics Covered

- Agile Software Development
- Agile Manifesto
- Agile Principles
- Agile Mindset
- Iterative and Incremental Development
- Scrum Framework
- Scrum Roles
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Development Team
- Product Backlog
- Sprint Backlog
- User Stories
- Story Points
- Acceptance Criteria
- Sprint Planning
- Daily Stand-up
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective

## Key Learnings

- Understood the fundamental concepts and principles of Agile software development.
- Learned how Agile focuses on iterative development and continuous improvement.
- Understood the four values of the Agile Manifesto and the importance of customer collaboration.
- Learned about Scrum and how work is organized into short development cycles called Sprints.
- Understood the responsibilities of the Product Owner, Scrum Master, and Development Team.
- Learned how Product Backlogs and Sprint Backlogs are used to organize project requirements.
- Understood how User Stories describe requirements from the user's perspective.
- Learned how Story Points are used to estimate the effort required for development tasks.
- Understood the purpose of Sprint Planning, Sprint Review, and Sprint Retrospective.

## Outcome

Gained an understanding of Agile software development methodology and the fundamental practices of the Scrum framework.
