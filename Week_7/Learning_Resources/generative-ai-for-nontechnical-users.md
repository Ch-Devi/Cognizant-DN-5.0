# Generative AI - For Nontechnical Users

## Topics Covered

- Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
- Introduction to Generative AI
- How Generative AI Works
- Large Language Models
- AI Chatbots
- Text Generation
- Content Creation
- Text Summarization
- Translation
- Brainstorming
- Image Generation
- AI-Assisted Productivity
- Prompt Engineering Basics
- Writing Effective Prompts
- Reviewing AI-Generated Content
- AI Limitations
- AI Hallucinations
- Privacy and Data Security
- Responsible Use of AI

## Key Learnings

- Understood the basic concepts of Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI without requiring advanced technical knowledge.
- Learned how Generative AI can be used for everyday tasks and professional activities.
- Understood how AI tools can assist with writing, summarization, translation, brainstorming, and content creation.
- Learned the basics of interacting with AI chatbots using clear instructions and prompts.
- Understood how providing proper context can improve the quality of AI-generated responses.
- Learned that AI-generated information should be reviewed and verified before being used.
- Understood the risks related to incorrect information, privacy, and data security.
- Learned how to use Generative AI tools in a responsible and productive manner.

## Outcome

Gained practical knowledge of Generative AI and learned how to use AI tools effectively and responsibly for everyday and professional tasks.
