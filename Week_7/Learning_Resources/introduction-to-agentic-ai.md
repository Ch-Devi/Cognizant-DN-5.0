## Key Learnings

- Understood the concept of Agentic AI and how it differs from traditional AI systems.
- Learned that AI agents can work toward specific goals by reasoning, planning, and taking actions.
- Understood how complex tasks can be divided into smaller steps by an AI agent.
- Learned about the importance of memory and context in agent-based systems.
- Understood how AI agents can interact with external tools and services to complete tasks.
- Learned about the concept of autonomous decision-making and multi-step task execution.
- Gained an understanding of single-agent and multi-agent systems.
- Learned about the potential applications and challenges of Agentic AI.
- Understood the importance of safety, human oversight, and responsible development of autonomous AI systems.

## Outcome

Gained a foundational understanding of Agentic AI and learned how intelligent AI agents can reason, plan, use tools, and perform multi-step tasks to achieve specific goals.
