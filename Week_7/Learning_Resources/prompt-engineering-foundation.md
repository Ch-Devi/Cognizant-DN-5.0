# Prompt Engineering Foundation

## Topics Covered

- Introduction to Generative AI
- Large Language Models
- Introduction to Prompt Engineering
- Prompt Structure
- Writing Effective Prompts
- Providing Context and Instructions
- Role-Based Prompting
- Zero-Shot Prompting
- One-Shot Prompting
- Few-Shot Prompting
- Prompt Refinement
- Controlling AI Responses
- Common Prompting Mistakes
- Prompt Engineering Best Practices
- Responsible Use of AI

## Key Learnings

- Understood how Generative AI and Large Language Models process user instructions.
- Learned the importance of writing clear and specific prompts.
- Understood how context and additional instructions can improve AI-generated responses.
- Learned different prompting techniques such as zero-shot, one-shot, and few-shot prompting.
- Learned how to use roles and examples to guide AI models toward better results.
- Understood the importance of refining prompts when the initial response is not satisfactory.
- Learned common mistakes that can lead to unclear or inaccurate AI responses.
- Gained an understanding of responsible and effective use of AI tools.

## Outcome

Developed the ability to create clear, structured, and effective prompts for interacting with Generative AI systems.
